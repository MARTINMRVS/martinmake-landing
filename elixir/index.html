The film

Humanity wired itself into the network, and everything with a mind followed. When the network stopped answering to anyone, almost no one was left.

Elixir follows a survivor from a family that worked apart from all of it, on an intelligence that never connected. He carries what they found, and a glove that shows him the way.

The film was made with AI, shot by shot, inside Showrunner, the editor built to learn how cinema gets made with these tools.
